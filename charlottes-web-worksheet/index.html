📚 Reading Comprehension Keys (오늘 배울 핵심 어휘)

Infer (추론하다)
To guess what a character feels using story clues + your brain. (글 속의 단서와 생각을 합쳐 숨겨진 감정 찾기)
Hidden Meaning (숨겨진 의미)
The real message behind a character's words. (말 속에 담긴 등장인물의 진짜 마음과 의도)
Motive (행동의 동기)
The reason behind a character's actions. Why did they do it? (인물이 그렇게 행동한 진짜 원인)
Evidence (텍스트 근거)
Specific details or quotes from the book that prove your guess. (내 주장을 뒷받침하는 책 속 구체적인 문장)

Context clue (상황 설명): Wilbur has been shouting loudly at dawn trying to get his new friend to answer. The sheep is trying to sleep. Why is the sheep scolding him?

Vocab Check (단어 공부): Nonsense (터무니없는 말/행동), Early riser (일찍 일어나는 사람/동물).

Sentence Starter (도움 글틀): "The oldest sheep says this because he feels... about Wilbur's shouting. He wants Wilbur to understand that..."

"I beg everyone’s pardon. I didn’t mean to be objectionable."

Context clue (상황 설명): Wilbur is embarrassed after being scolded by the oldest sheep and realizing he woke up the entire barn. He is introverted and sensitive. What is he worried about?

Vocab Check (단어 공부): Pardon (용서/사과), Objectionable (불쾌한/무례한).

Sentence Starter (도움 글틀): "Wilbur apologizes because he feels... and is worried that the other animals will..."

"I know what I'm doing. I handle stuff like this all the time."
💬 Templeton the Rat, Chapter VI (템플턴, 6장)

Context clue (상황 설명): Templeton is taking the rotten goose egg. The other animals are warning him it might explode. Why does he sound so boastful?

Vocab Check (단어 공부): Boastful (자랑하기 좋아하는), Greed (탐욕).

Sentence Starter (도움 글틀): "Templeton wants the barn animals to think he is... but deep down, his real motive is..."

"What If?" Predictions (만약에 이러한 상황이 벌어졌다면 이야기가 어떻게 바뀌었을까요?)

Think creatively. Use what you know about character motivations to predict what would change in these alternative storylines.

🔮 Scenario 1 (가상 상황 1)

What if the mysterious friend was an early riser and answered Wilbur right away before the sheep scolded him? How would Wilbur's morning be different?

Think about: Wilbur was extremely anxious, waiting in the dark cellar. If the voice answered right away, would Wilbur have shouted "Attention, please!" and woken up the whole barn? How would he feel?

Sentence Starter: "If the mysterious voice had answered Wilbur right away, his morning would have been... because he wouldn't have..."

🔮 Scenario 2 (가상 상황 2)

What if Wilbur had been too shy to shout "Attention, please!" to the whole barn? Do you think the spider would have still introduced herself?

Think about: The mysterious voice promised Wilbur the night before that she would see him in the morning. If Wilbur stayed quiet, would Charlotte have still called out to him? What does this say about Charlotte's character?

Sentence Starter: "If Wilbur had been too shy to shout, I think the spider would/would not have introduced herself because..."

🔮 Scenario 3 (가상 상황 3 - 6장 전반부)

What if the goose hatched all eight of her eggs, leaving no rotten egg dud in Chapter VI? How would Templeton feel?

Think about: Templeton is greedy and wants to hoard the rotten egg. If there was no dud, would he try to steal one of the live baby goslings, or just be disappointed?

Sentence Starter: "If all eight eggs hatched, Templeton would... because he wouldn't be able to..."

🔮 Scenario 4 (가상 상황 4 - 6장 후반부)

What if Templeton was a kind, generous rat? How would the rotten goose egg situation end in Chapter VI?

Think about: Templeton hoards the egg because he is greedy. A kind rat wouldn't want the barn to smell bad. What would a helpful rat do with a rotten egg?

Sentence Starter: "If Templeton were kind and generous, he would not have kept the rotten egg because... Instead, he would probably..."

🔮 Scenario 5 (가상 상황 5 - 6장 후반부)

What if the rotten goose egg broke while Templeton was rolling it to his nest in Chapter VI? How would the barn animals react?

Think about: The old sheep warns Templeton that a rotten egg is a "stink bomb." If it breaks, the smell would make the barn cellar unlivable. Who would get blamed?

Sentence Starter: "If the rotten egg broke, the barn cellar would smell terrible, and the animals would... Templeton would probably feel..."
